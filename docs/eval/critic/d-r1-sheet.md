# Behavior sheet, system layer, round 1

Scope and limits of the evidence:
- The rig (one Raspberry Pi 5 with its 800x480 panel) had **no sensors, no relays, no fan tach, no buzzer and no alarm relay attached**. Every real-mode run shows a dead temperature/humidity sensor.
- "Output", "buzzer" and "contact" states on the rig come **only from the board's own GPIO registers** (pinctrl `op dl`/`op dh`) or from the application's status JSON. No relay contact, no buzzer sound and no wire was measured.
- In the captures, GPIO24 is high exactly when status says `alarm_contact_closed=true`, and GPIO25 is high exactly when `buzzer_on=true`. The pin names below rely on that match (INFERRED).
- All captures from 13:14 to 16:05 UTC on 2026-11-07, and the reproductions at 16:12-16:14, ran one cured binary: sha256 2ff50a33…, version string a293cfd79079cec6.

Items:

- Item R2.4: On the real board, the controller process starts 20.2-23.3 s after power-on after a clean reboot (3 captures). After a mains cut it starts at 36.8 s, with the kernel at 19.3 s and hardware bring-up at 33.8 s. The first control step comes 0.94-1.04 s after process start, at 21.1-24.2 s and 37.8 s of uptime. No sensor was attached, so "back to band" never happens on the rig: temperature and humidity stay FAULT. In the simulated plant, after a mains cut the process waits 57.7-68.7 s before its first control step, at 89.6-106.2 s of uptime. The status then reports temperature recovery times of 420 s (1x), 927 s (compressed) and 2347 s (full cure, simulated seconds). (MEASURED-RIG for start timing; MEASURED-SIM for back to band; NOT-SHOWN for back to band on real hardware)
- Item R3.3: After mains return and after every process restart, the panel shows cooling held by a 4:00 power-on delay. Examples: 3:21 left at 77 s of uptime after the cut, then 2:49 left 32 s later. After a SIGKILL restart it reads 3:57-3:58 left. Heat and humidifier show "settling after power-on, 5:00". Only the screen and the GPIO registers were seen: GPIO17/22/23/27 stayed low throughout. Heat and humidifier were also blocked by the sensor fault, so the hold has no independent output-level demonstration. At 700x simulation speed the hold is under 1 s of real time, and "COOL ON" plus "HEAT ON" appear on the first screen after the cut. (MEASURED-RIG, panel and registers only, no relays attached)
- Item R3.4: A protection hold shows as an amber "COOL HOLD" chip in the output row. The bottom ticker reads "COOL HELD: power-on delay, m:ss left" with a live countdown. Faulted outputs show red "HEAT FAULT"/"HUMID FAULT" chips, and the ticker reads "HEAT, HUMID HELD: temperature data is bad". The simulated state also lists holds with reason and remaining seconds, for example cool held for defrost with 774 s remaining. (MEASURED-RIG for the panel; MEASURED-SIM for the state record)
- Item R4.1: With nothing on the sensor pin, temperature and RH tiles read "FAULT / no valid reading". At 2 s of process age the status has no sensor alarm yet, and the restart-loop screen shows "no reading yet". By 38-40 s, SENSOR_CLIMATE_FAULT (critical) and SENSOR_AIR_FAULT (warning) are active, buzzer_on=true and GPIO25 is high. FAN_TACH (warning) appears between 64 s and 126 s, with the fan PWM at 40-45 % and 0 rpm. The alarm contact is never armed (alarm_armed=false) and GPIO24 stays low, which signals alarm. The banner says heater and humidifier are off and "cooling repeats its recent pattern with no feedback". With nothing attached, the pattern could not be checked. This held unchanged for 370 s and across 6 reboots or power-ups. (MEASURED-RIG, no sensors attached)
- Item R4.5: On the real board only setpoints were present, and 13.0 °C / 75 % shows before and after the cut. No program was running on real hardware across any power loss. In the simulated plant, the 12-day test program continued in the same phase after the cut. For 1x, total elapsed went from 177324 s to 221603 s, setpoints stayed 14 °C / 82 %, and degree-hours were marked incomplete. Compressed went from 191912 s to 350231 s. Alarm history (since, cleared, peak, duration) carried across the cut. (MEASURED-SIM; NOT-SHOWN on real hardware)
- Item R4.6: After a real mains cut, the board's RTC read epoch 90, so it kept no time. The system clock came up about 2.3 min behind real time. The app set clock_trusted=false and raised a CLOCK_UNTRUSTED info alarm, and the panel clock showed "~14:43:47?". Journal entries from the new boot (14:43:06) carry times earlier than the last pre-cut capture (14:43:52). NTP synced within about 3.3 min, and the clock then read trusted. After warm reboots the RTC kept time and NTP was unsynced at 60 s, yet clock_trusted=true. Behavior with no network was not tested. (MEASURED-RIG)
- Item R4.7: Real cut: outage "at" 14:43:19 UTC, although status showed the process alive at 14:43:52 and pings answered until 14:44:03.5. Duration first read 0 with duration_ok=false, then resolved to 125 s at 3 min 17 s after boot, once NTP synced. temp_known=false, but temp_on_back_c and peak_temp_c are written as 0, and recover_sec is -1. Process crashes also create outage records: 6-23 s recorded for real gaps of 2.2-12.6 s. In the reproduction, "at" equals the previous process's start time, 11 s before the hang began. Simulated 1x: 7321 s, 14.5 °C on return, peak 15 °C, recovered in 420 s, while the simulator's own accounting shows 837 s of plant time. Compressed: 93058 s against 92870 s accounted, 21.5 °C on return. (MEASURED-RIG for timestamps, no temperature on the rig; MEASURED-SIM for temperatures)
- Item R4.9: Status reports log_ok=true, watchdog_ok=true and storage_free_mb ≈ 2380. System alarms observed: CONTROLLER_RESTART_LOOP (critical, on the 4th start within 600 s), OUTPUTS_DROPPED (critical), CLOCK_UNTRUSTED (info), and DISPLAY_MISSING (critical, raised only by a planted marker file). No storage fault was injected: no full disk, read-only filesystem, corruption or write error on the card. Clean stops logged "stop is slow: still flushing state to the card" and took 5.5, 6.7, 11.1 and 22.75 s. Goroutine dumps at watchdog kills show threads inside fsync. (MEASURED-RIG for stop durations; NOT-SHOWN for storage-fault reporting)
- Item R4.11: App crash (SIGKILL, real): the process exits in 81-90 ms, and the guard logs "outputs off confirmed" at about 130 ms. GPIO25 (buzzer) drops from high to low within 23 ms of the trigger. "PANEL STOPPED" shows by about 1.1 s, and the service is back in 2.7 s (3 of 4 runs; one took 7.9 s). SIGABRT: back in 2.7-2.8 s. In the simulated plant with the contact closed, GPIO24 opens at 0.9-1.8 s. An injected control panic opens it at 0.03-0.11 s. Hang (SIGSTOP): the guard drops GPIO24 and GPIO25 at 2.96-3.0 s, systemd's watchdog sends SIGABRT at 9.2-10.0 s, and the service is back at 11.9-13.0 s. Real-mode captures 30-* began with every line already low, so they show no drop. Kernel panic (simulated plant, contact closed first): the host was unreachable for 117-123 s by host clock (the script printed "56 s"). On reboot the lines are unconfigured with pull-down until 33-35 s of uptime, then low, and the contact re-closed by 114 s. No line was observed during the panic itself. Power loss: lines are low after return, and the outage period itself cannot be observed. The PID 1 hardware watchdog (10 s) and a guard-daemon crash were never exercised. (MEASURED-RIG, registers only, no relays attached; MEASURED-SIM for contact-closed cases; NOT-SHOWN for kernel hang and guard crash)
- Item R5.4: Sensor-fault alarms are not suppressed after power-on: critical with buzzer by 38-40 s of process age, warning (fan tach) by about 2 min. POWER_OUTAGE is raised immediately on every start, even after a clean restart, at severity info, warning or critical depending on the run. In the simulated plant the contact closed by 114 s after a panic reboot, then reopened at about 162 s when RH_HIGH (warning) fired. Deviation alarms use an alarm delay of 900 s and a door delay of 300 s per the limits record. No capture isolates a separate power-on suppression window for deviation alarms. (MEASURED-RIG for sensor alarms; MEASURED-SIM for delays; NOT-SHOWN for a post-power-on deviation-alarm window)
- Item R5.5: The local signal is GPIO25, high whenever buzzer_on=true. The panel shows a red banner and a "SILENCE BUZZER" button. No buzzer was attached. In the simulated cure, a silence command at day 8.303 got an "ok" reply, but buzzer_on stayed true and buzzer_silenced stayed false at 8.306 and 8.342. The buzzer went quiet only after the acknowledgement at 8.35 cleared the alarm. One real-mode status showed buzzer_on=false while a critical sensor alarm was active, and the next showed true. The signal goes silent whenever the app dies or hangs: 0.02-2.7 s on a kill and 3.0-12.6 s on a hang in the reproduction. It also goes silent through any power loss. Silence-and-return on a new alarm is only a unit test. (MEASURED-RIG registers, no buzzer attached; MEASURED-SIM; TEST)
- Item R5.7: Healthy: GPIO24 is high (contact closed) only once armed by a good temperature and with no warning or critical alarm active. Info-level alarms leave it closed. Alarming: GPIO24 goes low on a critical alarm (climate sensor dead, display marker) and also on a warning (RH_HIGH). Dead: low on SIGKILL (0.9-1.8 s), on a control panic (0.03-0.11 s), on a hang (about 3.0 s), and with the service stopped. Unpowered: no measurement is possible. At boot the pin is unconfigured with pull-down until about 33 s, then driven low. On the rig, with no sensor, it never closed in any real-mode run. After a restart it may not re-close for over 150 s while alarms persist. The relay's own fail-open behavior is INFERRED, since no relay was attached. (MEASURED-RIG registers; MEASURED-SIM for the closed state; INFERRED for unpowered)
- Item R5.9: No capture shows the controller's own periodic data record. Exported state has "trend": null in all three cure runs, and the TREND screen was never captured. Visible: alarm history with since, cleared, peak and duration, persisted across a simulated cut. A persistent system journal (128 MB cap, SyncIntervalSec=5m) holds service lifecycle lines but not sensor-alarm transitions. The timeline.jsonl files are the test harness polling the controller, not the controller's own record. (NOT-SHOWN for periodic sensor recording; MEASURED-SIM for alarm history)
