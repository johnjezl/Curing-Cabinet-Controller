# Behavior sheet, round 1

All numbers come from a simulated plant with one lumped air node. No hardware was involved. "Median / worst" means across 36 simulated plants, read from sweep/metrics_long.csv. Raw samples exist only for the nominal plant and for one worst plant per scenario, so sweep values for other plants could not be recomputed. "Nominal" means the base plant only. Temperatures are errors from setpoint unless marked absolute. Spans are p99-p1. "Excl. defrost" leaves out defrost and the 40 min after it.

## R1 Regulation

- Item R1.1: Setpoints exercised are 1.5 to 25 C, 55 to 90 %RH and 1.0 to 1.2 m/s airflow. No raw run shows a limit being enforced or a value rejected. Only a passing test named for command validation exists, with no values visible. (NOT-SHOWN)
- Item R1.2: Probe-reading span excl. defrost, nominal / median / worst: 13 C 0.92 / 1.28 / 5.39 C; 14 C 0.84 / 1.23 / 3.11 C; 4 C 1.06 / 1.23 / 3.71 C; 1.5 C 1.54 / 1.69 / 3.94 C; 24 C 3.31 / 2.49 / 4.69 C; 20 C 3.24 / 2.59 / 7.64 C; 25 C 2.29 / 2.33 / 8.94 C. Setpoints of 14 C and below stay under 2 C at the median, and no setpoint stays under 2 C on all plants. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R1.3: Heating below setpoint uses a heater that was never commanded on with a bad temperature reading (0 s across all fault runs on all plants). Heater duty at 20 C is median 64%, worst 96%. At 25 C it saturates at 96%, and true air still averages -0.85 C at the median and -1.55 C at worst, with product -1.99 C / -2.57 C. At 13 C the heater runs 34% duty against the compressor's 35% (median). Closures are median 176/day and worst 675/day. Cures logged up to about 10,700 heater closures. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R1.4: Humidity is raised by a humidifier and lowered only by compressor plus reheat. The plant has no dehumidifier. True RH mean error excl. defrost at 13 C is median -0.07, range -3.0 to +2.9 %RH. At 25 C / 55 %RH it is median +10.4, worst +27.0. At 1.5 C worst is -12.9. At 4 C worst is -15.8. The reported 15 min mean error at 13 C is nominal 7.92, median 7.12, worst 11.17, but it is inflated by partial averaging windows. A full-window recomputation, excl. defrost, gives 3.27 on the nominal plant and 9.52 on plant s24. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R1.5: True air span excl. defrost, nominal / median / worst: 13 C 1.19 / 1.62 / 5.55 C; 4 C 1.35 / 1.86 / 4.10 C; 1.5 C 1.73 / 2.12 / 4.33 C; 24 C 3.56 / 2.77 / 4.89 C; 20 C 3.56 / 2.81 / 8.50 C; 25 C 2.56 / 2.65 / 9.41 C. Mean error is within about 0.5 C at 13, 14 and 24 C on all plants. Worst means are +1.03 C at 4 C and +3.39 C at 1.5 C on the hot-room plant, and -1.55 C at 25 C. The single air node cannot show differences between locations in the cabinet. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R1.6: True RH span excl. defrost, median / worst: 13 C 18.9 / 31.2; 14 C 17.3 / 27.8; 4 C 18.5 / 39.4; 1.5 C 20.5 / 40.6; 24 C 15.4 / 23.8; 20 C 23.5 / 37.1; 25 C 24.5 / 34.8 %RH. Nominal at 13 C is 22.1 %RH. Over a nominal 52-day phase at 13 C / 76 %RH, RH p1/p99 is -9.7 / +6.2 and min/max is -16.2 / +11.9. On the nominal plant the 5 min mean does not stay inside +/-5 %RH in routine operation. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R1.8: Calibration offsets are set by command at start and survived 280 of 280 simulated power cuts. A test for tare and calibrate through commands passes. No in-service calibration procedure was run. A biased-sensor plant passes its bias straight into true air (-0.50 C) and true RH (+2.9 %RH), and nothing flags it. (TEST)
- Item R1.9: Airflow mean error is median +0.01 m/s, worst +0.33 m/s. Span is median 0.47 m/s, worst 0.87 m/s. Nominal fan duty is about 14.6%, and 25% in defrost. A dead air sensor alarms in 5 s and a stalled fan in 28 to 30 s. During a stall, nominal true air reaches -4.0 / +1.1 C and RH +20 %RH. On the worst plant air reaches +8.8 C and -5.8 C, and RH +24 %RH. Airflow at the product surface is not modelled separately from the manifold value. (MEASURED-SIM)

## R2 Recovery

- Item R2.1: Door peak true air error, median / worst: 30 s at 13 C 1.93 / 5.79 C; 2 min at 13 C 2.05 / 6.56 C; 10 min at 13 C 2.71 / 7.88 C; 30 s at 4 C 3.55 / 9.06 C; 2 min at 4 C 4.68 / 10.83 C. Reported first return to +/-2 C: 2 min at 13 C 0 / 295 s; 10 min at 13 C 45 / 450 s; 2 min at 4 C 152 / 605 s. Recomputed time until the 3 min mean stays inside +/-2 C for an hour is close on the nominal plant, but it reaches 2945 s on plant s21 against 140 s reported. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.2: With a door switch, cooling, heating and humidifying all read 0 while the door is open, and the fan runs at 11 to 13% (nominal, door runs of 30 s, 2 min and 10 min). Behavior for a door open longer than 10 min is not run. On plants without a door switch (8 of 36) regulation continues with the door open and no door alarm is raised. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.3: Reported defrost temperature recovery to +/-2 C, median / worst: 13 C 0 / 265 s; 4 C 0 / 0 s; 1.5 C 345 / 925 s, with one plant null. The clock for those figures starts at a fixed 1800 s after defrost start, up to 1394 s after the real end. From the real end on the nominal plant, return to +/-1 C takes 1254 s at 13 C, 1324 s at 4 C and 434 s at 1.5 C. On the hot-room plant at 1.5 C the 3 min mean never settled within +/-2 C in 3600 s. Peak true air is median +3.46 C, worst +5.96 C at 1.5 C. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.4: On power-on the compressor is held 240 s. Empty pulldown to within 1 C at 13 C is median 668 s, worst 2305 s. A nominal loaded start at 13 C reaches +/-1 C at 975 s, and 3625 s at 4 C. A 1.5 C start takes 5580 s. Restart after outages is covered under R3.3. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.5: Empty pulldown to within 1 C of 4 C is nominal 1635 s, median 1635 s, p95 3740 s, worst 7935 s (hot room, weak compressor). (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.6: A 10 kg product returns to within 1 C of setpoint in median 3168 s, worst 5600 s at 13 C, and median 4120 s, worst 8735 s at 4 C. Product is one lump with a fixed coupling (1 h time constant) that is not swept. These times are not evidence for dense whole pieces. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.7: Loading warm product peaks true air at median +2.40 C, worst +6.34 C at 13 C, and median +4.87 C, worst +10.03 C at 4 C. Product peaks at median +3.26 C, worst +5.77 C, and median +3.93 C, worst +6.64 C. Air first returns to +/-2 C in median 0 s, worst 580 s at 13 C, and median 0 s, worst 2700 s at 4 C. Nominal air takes about 1600 to 1800 s to stay within +/-1 C. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.8: Reported RH recovery to +/-8 %RH, median / worst: 2 min door at 13 C 0 / 475 s; 10 min door at 13 C 150 / 945 s; 2 min door at 24 C 362 / 665 s. Many zeros appear because a trailing mean is tested once. Recomputed time to stay inside +/-8 %RH on the nominal plant: 2 min door at 4 C 625 s (reported 0); defrost 839 to 1404 s (reported 0); warm load at 4 C 3420 s (reported 0). Several worst plants did not settle inside +/-8 %RH within 3600 s. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.9: The humidifier first switches on at 301 s while air is still about 20 C. RH peaks in the first 3 h on the nominal plant at 90.3 %RH (set 76), 95.1 %RH (set 85) and 94.7 %RH (set 90), and 83.6 %RH with no humidifying at set 55. Reaching +/-5 %RH takes 315 s at 13 C, 1950 s at 24 C, 4505 s at 4 C and 5155 s at 1.5 C. (MEASURED-SIM)

## R3 Compressor and defrost

- Item R3.1: Minimum compressor off time is 240 s, recomputed from event logs on 154 raw runs and read for every sweep run. No violation was seen. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R3.2: Minimum on is 120 s, minimum start spacing is 480 s, and at most 8 starts occur in any rolling hour, all on every sweep run. In normal operation it runs against this limit: median 7.27 starts/h at 13 C, and 9622 starts in a nominal 61-day cure (about 158/day). (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R3.3: First compressor start is never earlier than 240 s after power-on, across all sweep runs and 280 outages of 5 s to 23.8 h. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R3.5: On a lost temperature reading the compressor runs open loop, with heater and humidifier off. Duty on the nominal plant is 15% at 13 C, 35% at 4 C and 0% at 24 C, against prior duties of 33%, 28% and 7.5%. Over 24 h at 13 C, true air min is median -4.1 C, worst -11.0 C, and max is median +0.5 C, worst +5.6 C. At 4 C, min is median -5.0 C, worst -8.4 C (absolute -4.4 C), and max is median +1.2 C, worst +14.3 C. Product reaches absolute -3.9 C to +15.9 C. Air went below 0 C on 26 of 36 plants and product on 24 of 36 at the 4 C setpoint. The nominal plant reached air -1.06 C and product -0.7 C. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R3.6: Defrost is scheduled about every 12.3 h at 13 C, 8.3 h at 4 C and 6.5 h at 1.5 C. The cap is 1200 s at 13 C and 4 C, and 1800 s at 1.5 C. It ends on time or on an air temperature limit. On the hot-room plant it ended after 1 to 973 s and frost reached 592 g at 4 C. Frost peaks at median 24.5 g, worst 1079 g at 4 C, and median 73.8 g, worst 987 g at 1.5 C (plant s08). The schedule does not adapt to frost: at 13 C it still runs 20 min with only about 3 g of frost on the nominal plant. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R3.7: There is no fan delay after defrost. The fan runs at 25% during defrost, 11% immediately after, and about 14.6% at 3 min (nominal and hot-room plants). The compressor restarts 120 to 192 s after defrost ends. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R3.8: Condenser protection does not appear in any run, alarm code or plant parameter. (NOT-SHOWN)

## R4 Faults and power loss

- Item R4.1: Lost readings are shown as: climate sensor 19 s on all plants; air sensor 5 s; scale 60 s; a stuck temperature 356 to 2007 s (median 741 s); a stuck RH 834 to 4328 s (median 1677 s). (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.2: With lost or suspect temperature or humidity, the heater and humidifier were on for 0 s across all plants, and the compressor falls to the open-loop duty in R3.5. Temperature and RH come from one sensor, so both are lost together. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.3: With RH alone lost, true air on the nominal plant stays near setpoint (mean +0.16 C), the compressor falls to about 8% duty and the humidifier is off. True RH then averages +11.8 %RH, peaking near +17 %RH over 8 h. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.4: Sensor fault alarms clear 9 to 19 s after the climate sensor recovers. Regulation returns to +/-2 C in 65 s at 13 C, 175 s at 24 C and 435 s at 4 C on the nominal plant, and 14515 s on the hot-room plant at 4 C. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.5: Setpoint, limits, calibration and active program all survived 280 of 280 simulated outages. Program progress lost was at most 60 s per cut, never negative. Outage time is not counted toward phase time. The phase was unchanged in 279 of 280. 400 of 400 injected storage-operation power cuts recovered state and lost no durable lines, with a single seed. 30 of 30 process kills recovered, with no regression in elapsed time. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.7: Outages are recorded in 280 of 280. With a trusted clock, duration is within 59 s. Without one, duration is reported as 0 in 125 of 140 outages, and the 15 reported are within 59 s. Temperature on return is within 0.53 C of true air and recorded peak within 0.44 C. Humidity on return is not recorded. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.8: Alarms that do not involve a sensor or fan do not change regulation on the nominal plant: storage faults left mean air error at -0.01 C, and scale faults left the same air range (-0.5 to +0.9 C) as a run with no effective fault. A stalled fan or a sensor fault does change it, by forcing outputs off or open-loop. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.9: Storage faults (full, write failure) alarm in 61 s and clear 601 s after repair, and regulation continues with mean air error -0.01 C on the nominal plant. Tests for torn writes, corrupt state and stalled storage pass. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.10: Plausible-but-wrong readings are caught unevenly. Stuck and implausible readings were detected on 36 of 36. A fast temperature drift of about 1 C/h was detected during the 12 h fault on 1 of 36 plants (flagged detected on 35 only because alarms fired after repair), with no alarm on the nominal plant while air fell to -12.4 C and product to -10.7 C. A slow temperature drift was detected on 22 of 36 in 10 days, median at 7.1 days, and never on 14 plants, with air to -7.4 C. A slow RH drift was detected on 36 of 36, median 3 days, worst 7.1 days, with true RH down to -35 %RH. Drift was tested only in the direction that makes the cabinet cold or dry. Drift in the warm or wet direction is not run. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.11: No certified, listed or rated status appears in any evidence. (NOT-SHOWN)

## R5 Alarms

- Item R5.1: Observed alarm kinds include temperature and humidity high and low, humidity not falling, cooling, heating and humidifier without effect, heat and cool stuck on, sensor faults and suspect readings for temperature, RH, air and scale, erratic readings, drift suspect, fan stall and tach, airflow blocked, door open, door switch fault, output fault, storage fault, power outage, program finished, clock untrusted, and calibration check due. Severities are info, warning and critical. Temperature high and low are warning only. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R5.2: Temperature high fired at reading error +2.25 to +6.65 C (median +2.74 C), after 1090 to 4680 s above +2 C on the nominal plant. Temperature low fired at -2.6 to -5.0 C. Stored humidity limits are setpoint +/-10 %RH. A compressor failure on the nominal plant takes 4842 s to its first alarm, and the correct cooling alarm comes at 6840 s. Across plants the first expected alarm is median 4683 s, worst 21581 s, and 2 of 36 plants raise no expected alarm in 8 h. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R5.3: With a door switch, a door alarm fires at 301 s and clears at closing, in the one 10 min door run on the nominal plant. Doors of 2 min and under raise none. Plants with no switch have no door alarm, and a dead switch raises only an info alarm, at 31 s. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R5.4: No temperature alarm fired during a 10 min door opening on the nominal plant at +2.84 C, and passing tests cover door and delay suppression. On the nominal plant a humidity-not-falling alarm cleared exactly at defrost start (43201 s), with no change in RH, and rose again later, so defrost hides it. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R5.5: Warnings and criticals both sound the buzzer. Silence stopped it for 1795 s and it then resumed. Acknowledge stopped it until a new alarm arrived 943 s later. Silence and acknowledge do not clear the condition. In cures, which contain no acknowledge, the buzzer sounded for 81 to 94% of the time to recipe end. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R5.6: Sensor and temperature alarms clear themselves once the condition ends, in 1 s to about 30 min. A scale drift alarm clears at 21615 s and a humidifier-empty alarm did not clear within the run on the nominal plant. Power outage alarms stayed active up to weeks without acknowledge, until a later outage or a short-outage recovery. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R5.7: An alarm relay follows warnings and criticals. It stays on through silence and acknowledge while any alarm is active. It was on 81 to 94% of the time through every simulated cure, so a later alarm causes no change in the signal. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R5.8: Stored events include alarm, clear, boot, outage, defrost, phase and command records, with peak, limit, unit and duration. All cures and outage runs had 0 bad lines and 0 sequence gaps. Clear records for an alarm spanning a reboot have duration -1, and outage alarms store peak and limit 0.00 in C. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R5.10: The only reminder observed is a calibration check at info severity on day 30 of runs lasting past 30 days. Reminders for coil, drain, filter or water are not observed. (MEASURED-SIM)

## RF Food-related

- Item RF.1: Water activity is neither measured nor estimated. Weight loss is the only product indicator. (NOT-SHOWN)
- Item RF.2: No claim that food is safe is made in the run output. Finished recipes state that weight loss is a yield figure and that water activity and pH must be verified with calibrated instruments. It keeps holding conditions after finishing. Recipes do finish on time or weight without any safety check. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item RF.3: Indicated temperature matches true air with mean difference within 0.004 C and p99 absolute difference 0.58 to 1.05 C in cures. Indicated RH has p99 absolute difference 6.5 to 16.1 %RH. The cure plants carry no sensor bias, so this shows lag and noise, not accuracy; the sweep adds bias of at most 0.5 C and 3 %RH. Controller weight loss differs from true by 0.05 to 0.34 points, and by 1.58 points on the small plant. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item RF.4: Stored excursions are alarm records with peak and limit, including humidity, plus outage peak temperature. Excursions that do not reach an alarm get no record of their own. Those are only visible in per-minute minimum and maximum values. The biggest silent ones are the drift runs in R4.10. Outage records have no humidity. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item RF.5: The process record is one row per 60 s of temperature, RH and their min and max, air, weight, setpoints, output fractions, alarm count, recipe, phase, elapsed time and degree-hours above 15.6 C (for example 89139 rows over 61 days, 0 bad lines, 0 sequence gaps). Degree-hours match recomputation from readings: 403.4 reported vs 403.3 on the nominal 61-day cure. Nothing alarms on degree-hours. (MEASURED-SIM)
