# Front panel behavior sheet, round 1

Limits that apply to every item below:
- A framebuffer read is not the same as looking at the glass. MEASURED-RIG means the pixels were read back from the board's display memory. It says nothing about actual brightness, viewing angle, glare or whether the panel lit up.
- Every tap in the evidence was injected in software. None went through a real finger, glove or touch controller. The board's touch device was detected at start-up, but no physical touch was logged.
- Every board frame was driven by canned fixture data through a demo harness, not by real sensors or the real control core.
- Walkthrough timings use a virtual panel clock. A 3,600 s wait ran in under a second of wall time, so timeouts were not observed in real time on the board.
- Some items need more than one basis, so each clause is tagged where it differs. The closing parenthesis lists every basis used.
- Scale: 800 px is about 108 mm, so 1 px is about 0.135 mm, and 12 mm is about 89 px.

- Item R5.5: An active alarm shows a 98 px red banner with title, action text and clock, amber digits with a deviation (16.4 with +3.4), a red "ALARMS 1" nav button, and a 146 x 72 px (19.7 x 9.7 mm) SILENCE BUZZER button (MEASURED-RIG, rig/alarm.png). The banner color and title repeat in the header of every other screen, but only Home and Alarms have a silence button (MEASURED-SHOT). Silence is 1 tap from Home. Afterwards the button reads BUZZER SILENCED, and the banner, amber digits and nav count all stay (MEASURED-SHOT, walk/16). Acknowledge is 2 taps (ALARMS, then ACK or ACK ALL) with no hold; the row changes from NEW to ACKED and stays listed. Home is pixel-identical whether silenced or acknowledged (MEASURED-SHOT). Silence and acknowledge were never exercised on the board. No sound was captured anywhere, and whether the buzzer sounds or re-sounds is unknown (NOT-SHOWN). With several alarms, Home names the worst one and adds "+4" (MEASURED-SHOT). (MEASURED-RIG, MEASURED-SHOT, NOT-SHOWN)
- Item R5.8: The Alarms HISTORY tab lists 10 canned events at 4 per page over 3 pages. Each row shows name, severity, start and end time, duration, peak and limit, including humidity ("Humidity low ... peak 69 %, limit 72 %", "Humidity high ... peak 88 %, limit 84 %") (MEASURED-SHOT, normal__history.png). Rows do not show when an alarm was silenced or acknowledged. Pages 1 and 2 were seen; page 3 was not. An automated test of event types and times passes (TEST). With the real core the count is HISTORY 0, so no event produced by real alarm logic appears in the evidence (MEASURED-SHOT, realcore__alarms.png). No History screen exists among the board frames. Storage across restarts, capacity and export are unknown (NOT-SHOWN). (MEASURED-SHOT, TEST, NOT-SHOWN)
- Item R5.10: A maintenance reminder appears as a blue INFO banner, "Filter change due / Replace the air filter at your next stop", with a SILENCE BUZZER button and the same red "ALARMS 1" badge as a critical alarm. The row says "since Mar 14 08:22 (6 h 0 min)" and has an ACK button (MEASURED-SHOT, alarm-info__home.png, alarm-many__alarms-p3.png). No screen shows the service interval, hours used, how the reminder is set, or how it is cleared once the work is done (NOT-SHOWN). (MEASURED-SHOT, NOT-SHOWN)
- Item R5.11: Limits can be viewed without a code in 2 taps (MORE, HEALTH): "Temp alarms +2.0 / -2.0 °C, abs 20.0", "RH alarms +6 / -6 %". Active alarm rows also show the limit in force. Home shows no limits. Changing them takes about 10 taps from Home: MORE, INSTALLER, 4 digits, ENTER, ALARM LIMITS, +, SAVE. Save is a single tap. An unsaved value shows "was 2.0 (not saved)" and "Discarded in 60 s" (MEASURED-SHOT). Page 1 has temp high, temp low and humidity high. Page 2 has humidity low, alarm delay 15 min and door delay 5.0 min. Page 3 of 3 was never captured, so the absolute limit and airflow limit editors are unconfirmed (NOT-SHOWN). The walkthrough changed 2.0 to 2.5 and issued set_limits on the virtual clock, not on the board. (MEASURED-SHOT, NOT-SHOWN)
- Item R6.1: Normal Home has a green "OK: NO ALARMS, IN RANGE" banner with a seconds clock; temperature and humidity tiles with setpoints; air and weight tiles; recipe name, phase, time left and a loss bar; six output chips; six nav buttons; and a 100 x 5 px bar that moves along the bottom edge each second (MEASURED-RIG, rig/normal.png). The bar's meaning is explained only on Health (MEASURED-SHOT). After idling this view dims at about 600 s and drops to a sleep view at about 3,600 s, on the virtual clock (MEASURED-SHOT, walk/34, walk/35). The sleep view shows only grey "13.2 °C 78 %" and "no alarms. touch to wake" (MEASURED-RIG, rig/sleep.png, using shortened timers). Logged backlight is 102/255 dim and 25/255 sleep; the glass was not measured (MEASURED-RIG). Temperature digit contrast is 3.0:1 in dim and 3.4:1 in sleep (MEASURED-RIG). (MEASURED-RIG, MEASURED-SHOT)
- Item R6.2: Temperature digit height is 116 px, about 15.7 mm, on normal Home (y 105 to 220); 106 px, about 14.3 mm, with an alarm banner; and 53 px, about 7.2 mm, in sleep. Humidity matches at 117 px and 106 px. A bad temperature is replaced by the word STALE or FAULT at 40 to 41 px, about 5.5 mm. These are pixel sizes from display memory; physical size on the glass was not measured. (MEASURED-RIG)
- Item R6.3: With no recipe running, setpoints are viewed and changed on one screen: Home tile or SET, then + or -, then SAVE, which is 3 taps for one step (0.5 °C or 1 %RH), with + and - at 130 x 101 px (17.6 x 13.7 mm) (MEASURED-SHOT). Pending values are highlighted with "was 13.0 (not saved)" and "Not saved yet, Discarded in 30 s". Nothing is sent until SAVE, which is a single tap with no hold (walkthrough log, command only on SAVE). CANCEL discards. Timing out returns to Home with "Timed out. Change NOT saved; old value kept" at 31 s on the screen clock. An out-of-range request is refused with a toast, and the allowed range is not displayed beforehand (MEASURED-SHOT). Tests of commit, cancel, timeout and refusal pass (TEST). While a recipe runs, the screen is view-only and says to stop the cure first (MEASURED-SHOT); the shots do not show whether pausing permits edits (NOT-SHOWN). The board journal logged injected +, + and SAVE at 02:57:31 to 02:57:32, but no board frame shows the Set screen or the saved value (MEASURED-RIG, log only). (MEASURED-SHOT, TEST, MEASURED-RIG, NOT-SHOWN)
- Item R6.4: From Alarms history page 2 the panel returned to Home after a 31 s virtual wait, with the screen clock moving from 14:22:20 to 14:22:51 (MEASURED-SHOT, walk/20, walk/21). A test covering every screen passes (TEST). The timeout was not observed on the board in real time. Whether the installer session closes on timeout is unknown (NOT-SHOWN). (MEASURED-SHOT, TEST, NOT-SHOWN)
- Item R6.5: Locking is MORE plus a 2 s hold, and adds a roughly 96 x 36 px LOCKED chip. The lock survived a process restart. A 3.3 s hold unlocked it, with no code at any point (MEASURED-RIG, injected touches, rig/lock-set.png, rig/lock-after-restart.png, rig/lock-clear.png). A 1.3 s hold did not unlock it and showed "Released early" (MEASURED-SHOT, walk/28). While locked, Home, setpoints (view-only), the Cure table with controls disabled, and Health remain reachable; Defrost and Installer read "unlock first" (MEASURED-SHOT). Silence stays available while locked, per a passing test (TEST). No Alarms or Trend screen was captured while locked, and no locked alarm state was captured (NOT-SHOWN). A locked idle Home still says "Tap to choose a recipe" (MEASURED-RIG). (MEASURED-RIG, MEASURED-SHOT, TEST, NOT-SHOWN)
- Item R6.6: Installer settings sit behind MORE, INSTALLER and a numeric code of 4 to 8 digits. The default code forces a new code on first entry. A wrong code gives a toast and stays on the keypad. The menu has ALARM LIMITS, SENSOR OFFSETS, SCALE, CHANGE CODE and CLOSE INSTALLER (MEASURED-SHOT). Limits and offsets are shown as editable during a running cure; tare and calibrate are blocked during a cure with a stated reason (MEASURED-SHOT). A gate test passes (TEST). Lockout after repeated wrong codes is unknown (NOT-SHOWN). None of this was captured on the board. (MEASURED-SHOT, TEST, NOT-SHOWN)
- Item R6.7: Six recipes appear on 2 pages (Salami, Bresaola, Coppa, Pancetta, Lomo, and one user recipe marked "yours"). The detail screen shows a phase table of temperature, %RH, airflow and time or target (MEASURED-SHOT). Start is 4 taps: CURE, row, START..., START, with no hold. Pause and Resume are 2 taps with no confirmation. Next phase, Stop and End are CURE, button, then a 2 s hold; a 1.1 s hold is rejected (MEASURED-SHOT and walkthrough log). On the board an injected start was logged, and the frame shows phase 1 of 4 running (MEASURED-RIG, rig/walk-end.png). No recipe creation or editing appears on the panel (NOT-SHOWN). (MEASURED-SHOT, MEASURED-RIG, NOT-SHOWN)
- Item R6.8: Home and Cure show time left in the current phase ("15 d 21 h left in phase"). The phase arithmetic agrees with "Elapsed 14 d 3 h" (MEASURED-SHOT). The board frame shows the same Home text (MEASURED-RIG, rig/normal.png). When paused the line reads "PAUSED, clock stopped". An open-ended phase reads "until weight target or operator". Time left for the whole recipe and a finish date are not displayed anywhere (MEASURED-SHOT). The countdown was not observed advancing on real time. (MEASURED-RIG, MEASURED-SHOT)
- Item R6.9: During a program, Home does not show: whole-recipe time left, finish date, next-phase settings, loss rate, target weight in grams, door state unless a door alarm is active, or alarm limits. Cure adds the phase table and "%/day". Health adds door state and limits (MEASURED-SHOT). Temporary toasts, about 722 x 112 px, also cover the middle of the screen, including the air and weight tiles and output chips on Home, while they are up (MEASURED-SHOT, walk/15, walk/16). (MEASURED-SHOT)
- Item R6.10: The weight tile shows live grams and start weight ("4812 g, from 5880"). The loss bar reads "LOSS 18.2 % of 35", and 4812 from 5880 does equal 18.2 % (MEASURED-RIG, rig/normal.png). Cure adds "0.94 %/day", which does not equal 18.2 % over 14 d 3 h (1.29 %/day) and stays 0.94 at 31.4 % loss (MEASURED-SHOT). The end-of-cure frame shows 4812 from 5880 beside "LOSS 35.1 %", which is inconsistent (MEASURED-RIG, rig/end-of-cure.png). A bad scale shows FAULT, STALE or SUSPECT and "LOSS not current", with the bar removed (MEASURED-SHOT). Target weight in grams and an estimated finish are not displayed. No real load cell was involved in any board frame (MEASURED-RIG, fixture data only). (MEASURED-RIG, MEASURED-SHOT)
