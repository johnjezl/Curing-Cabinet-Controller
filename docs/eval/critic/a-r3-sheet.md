# Behavior sheet: cabinet control logic (round 3 evidence, evidence/a)

All of this evidence comes from a simulated plant written by the same team. It covers 36 plants plus a nominal "base" plant, one source hash (source_sha256 = source_sha256_end = f7a0ab9c…6997), and no hardware. "Median / worst" means across the 36 swept plants. I recomputed the numbers from raw samples.csv.gz and events.log where it says so. Every plant uses a coil floor of -28 C and a cooling capacity of roughly 130-520 W for a 0.2-0.6 m3 box. That is closer to a freezer than to a converted fridge, so cold-side harm is probably overstated and pull-down and recovery probably look better than they would on a real cabinet. Door openings are modelled gently: with a 60 min door opening at a 13 C setpoint the air rises by only 2.9 C.

- Item R1.1: The runs use temperature setpoints of 1.5, 4, 13, 14, 20, 24 and 25 C, humidity setpoints of 55-90 %RH and airflow setpoints of 1.0-1.5 m/s. The allowed limits and what happens to an out-of-range command are not visible in any run output. Only a unit test name (TestCommandsValidated) suggests that commands are validated. (NOT-SHOWN)
- Item R1.2: Spread of the filtered probe reading (p99-p1, excluding defrost plus 40 min). At cure 13 C: nominal 0.92 C, median 1.28 C, p95 4.49 C, worst 8.22 C (s03). At 14 C: median 1.20 C, worst 7.70 C. At aging 1.5 C: median 1.53 C, worst 3.77 C. At 4 C: median 1.26 C, worst 3.74 C. At 24 C: median 2.71 C, worst 5.19 C, and 3.27 C even on the nominal plant. I recomputed the nominal and s19 values from raw samples and they match. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R1.3: The heater runs when the room is colder than setpoint and as reheat during dehumidification. Heater duty saturates at about 96 % (biltong 25 C: median 96.1 %). At 25 C / 55 %RH, true air is below setpoint on most plants (mean -0.94 C nominal, median -0.98 C, worst -2.26 C). At 24 C in a 5 C room, lowest true air with power on and more than 2 h after a disturbance is -5.1 C from setpoint. The heater never ran while the temperature reading was bad: 0 s over 3096 runs. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R1.4: Largest deviation of the 15 min mean of true RH from setpoint, by recipe as median / worst. Cure 13 C: 3.65 / 11.4 %RH. Early 14 C: 3.74 / 7.4. Ferment 24 C: 3.49 / 8.4. Warm 20 C: 5.30 / 12.6. Beef 1.5 C: 6.09 / 26.6. Equalize 4 C: 4.43 / 39.4. Biltong 25 C / 55 %: 15.2 / 26.9, and 15.2 on the nominal plant. Biltong cannot be dehumidified to setpoint: mean RH +11.2 %RH at the median plant. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R1.5: Cabinet-level true air temperature span (p99-p1, excluding defrost plus 40 min). Cure 13 C: nominal 1.18 C, median 1.44 C, p95 4.71 C, worst 8.68 C. On s03 the air oscillates between 9.3 and 18.0 C in a limit cycle with heater and compressor on together. A simple thermostat on the same plant set has a worst case of 3.49 C. Beef 1.5 C: median 1.80 C, worst 4.18 C. Ferment 24 C: median 3.28 C, worst 5.45 C. Warm 20 C: median 3.09 C, worst 8.79 C. Mean offset outside defrost: median about 0.01 C, worst ±1.0 C (beef worst +3.4 C on the hot-room plant). (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R1.6: True RH swings widely on every compressor cycle even when the 15 min mean is close to setpoint. At cure 13 C the true RH span (p1-p99) is 22.1 %RH on the nominal plant, 21.1 median and 32.1 worst. Mean RH error by recipe as median / worst: cure -0.08 / ±5.1, beef -2.3 / -19.2, equalize -1.6 / -29.3, biltong +11.2 / +24.4. Humidifier relay closures reach 355/day in a 28-day run (10 312 total) and up to 428/day in the sweep. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R1.8: Calibration offsets are applied by command (set_calibration, logged at every boot). An operator reference check logs sensor minus reference and latches a critical REFERENCE_DISAGREES alarm when they differ (for example -24.2 C). A CAL_CHECK_DUE info message appears at 7 days. The sweep never applies an offset, so biased plants keep their bias of up to ±1 C and ±5 %RH. Nothing in the running system finds a static bias. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R1.9: Airflow at a 1.2 m/s setpoint: mean error median +0.012 m/s, worst +0.285 m/s; span (p1-p99) median 0.48 m/s, worst 0.87 m/s. After each defrost the fan restarts at 100 % duty, which on free-duct plants gives 6.5 m/s and an AIR_SUSPECT warning lasting about 1 min, with the alarm contact pulsing twice a day. A blocked duct is detected at 48-49 s and a fan stall at 29-30 s, both critical, on 36/36 plants. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.1: Door recovery: time for the 3 min mean of true air to return within ±2 C and stay there for 1 h. A 2 min door opening at 13 C gives a median of 180 s, p95 13 014 s and worst 22 455 s, and 1 of 36 plants never settles. Within ±1 C, 12 of 36 never settle (worst 24 795 s). RH within ±8 %RH: median 300 s, worst 5 940 s, 6 never settle. Peak air rise is +2.56 C median and +9.8 C worst at 13 C, and +5.0 C median and +13.2 C worst for 2 min at 4 C. The 180 s and 300 s values are the shortest the metric's averaging window can report. In the raw data, air is back in band within 5-90 s on the nominal plant. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.2: While the door is open, heater and humidifier are off and the fan drops to about 11 % duty. The compressor stays off for about the first 10 min, then cycles (36 % duty during a 60 min opening). DOOR_OPEN (warning) is raised at 301 s, with contact and buzzer, and clears when the door closes. No temperature alarm was raised during 10 min or 60 min openings at 13 C, where true air peaked at +2.8 and +2.9 C on the nominal plant. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.3: Defrost recovery: time for the 3 min mean within ±2 C, counted from the logged end of defrost. At 13 C: median 184 s, worst 3 649 s, 2 of 36 never settle. At 4 C: median 305 s, worst 23 487 s. At 1.5 C: median 419 s, worst 1 989 s. Within ±1 C at 13 C: median 1 184 s, worst 47 959 s, 11 never settle. Peak air during and after defrost: 13 C median +1.5 C, worst +5.6 C; 1.5 C median +3.6 C, worst +6.5 C. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.4: Time to recover after power returns (restart sets). Nominal plant: median 420 s, worst 1 880 s (no clock) or 3 021 s (with clock), 80/80 recovered. Small plant: median 513 s, worst 1 322 s. Hot-room weak-compressor plant: median 1 070 s, worst 4 463 s, and 14 of 60 restarts never recover because the plant cannot hold setpoint. The compressor's first start is never sooner than 240 s after boot. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.5: Empty-cabinet pull-down to within 1 C of setpoint. To 4 C: nominal 1 620 s, median 1 620 s, p95 6 329 s, worst 8 310 s. To 13 C: median 668 s, worst 3 450 s. These are likely optimistic for a fridge conversion because of the freezer-class coil (see header). (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.6: Loaded response: after a 90 s door opening and 10 kg of product at 20 C added to a 13 C cabinet, product returns within ±1 C at median 3 153 s and worst 8 475 s. Air peak is +2.46 C median and +8.42 C worst. The air ±1 C band settles at median 4 170 s, worst 42 640 s, and 6 of 36 never settle. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.7: Warm product recovery: 10 kg at 12 C added to a 4 C cabinet. Product returns within ±1 C at median 4 300 s and worst 14 980 s. Air peak is +5.1 C median and +12.2 C worst. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.8: Humidity recovery after defrost (5 min mean within ±8 %RH, held 1 h). At 13 C: median 1 164 s, worst 42 514 s, 6 never settle. At 4 C: median 1 359 s, worst 50 726 s, 7 never settle. At 1.5 C: median 2 759 s, worst 57 139 s, 8 never settle. Within ±5 %RH, 17-18 of 36 never settle. On the nominal plant the ±5 %RH result depends on how the window edge is handled (see defects). (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R2.9: The humidifier first switches on at 301 s after start in every run and pulses at about 7-9 % duty during pull-down. True RH first enters ±5 %RH at 315 s at 13 C / 76 %, at 5 255 s at 4 C / 85 % (nominal), and at 30 200 s on the worst equalize plant. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R3.1: The shortest compressor stop observed is 240 s across 3 077 sweep runs, and in 280 power cuts and 400 cut-matrix cases. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R3.2: Compressor starts are at least 480 s apart, with at most 8 starts in any rolling hour. The shortest commanded run is 120 s in normal operation. Runs of 1 s occur only when outputs are forced off by an output fault or guard-lease expiry (63 runs). (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R3.3: The compressor's first start is at 240 s after boot in every run; the minimum over 3 078 runs is 240 s. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R3.5: When the temperature reading is lost, the compressor keeps running on a pattern and the heater stays locked off. Over 24 h at 13 C, the median plant's largest rise is +1.1 C and the worst (s01) is a mean of +8.0 C with a peak of +11.8 C. Over 7 days at 13 C, s01 averages +12.0 C and product reaches about 25.5 C. At 1.5 C the median largest rise is +6.6 C (product +4.7 C) and the worst is +17.6 C (product +15.0 C). At 4 C the median is +4.0 C and the worst +14.4 C. On the cold side, at 1.5 C over 7 days on s14, air is below 0 C for 5.7 days and product for 6.8 days. At 24 C in a 5 C room the cabinet averages -18.6 C below setpoint. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R3.6: On the nominal plant, defrost is timed every 44 400 s (12.3 h) at 13 and 24 C, every 30 000 s (8.3 h) at 4 C and every 23 400 s (6.5 h) at 1.5 C. Each defrost lasts a fixed 1 200 s, or 1 800 s at 1.5 C; the longest observed is 1 800 s. It runs at 13 and 24 C even with zero frost. A defrost is ended within 1 s ("end: temperature limit") when the air is warm, and is then retried every 7 200 s. On hot-room plants this happens 82-112 times in 14 days and frost grows to 2.9-4.7 kg (6/36 plants at 1.5 C, 2/36 at 4 C). (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R3.7: After defrost ends, the fan is off for about 180 s, then cooling starts with a 100 % fan kick that falls to about 15 % duty within 60 s. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R3.8: No condenser or discharge sensing, alarm or protection appears in any output. (NOT-SHOWN)
- Item R4.1: A dead or unreadable temperature/humidity sensor raises a critical SENSOR_CLIMATE_FAULT at 19 s on 36/36 plants, at every setpoint. Heater and humidifier then run 0 s on bad data. The test name is TestSensorFaultShownWithin30sAndClears. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.2: When the reading returns, the alarm clears in median 9 s and worst 21-25 s. A critical TEMP_SUSPECT can appear for about 14 s on the first sample back. Regulation resumes on the reading. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.3: Settings survive power loss: setpoint, limits and calibration were retained in 280/280 random power cuts. In the cut matrix of 400 cuts at every write step, state and setpoint were recovered every time, boot succeeded and control ran. No durable log or event line was lost. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.4: Program (recipe) progress across power loss: outage time is not counted toward the program. At most 60 s of program time is lost per cut (total 1 629-2 621 s over 60-80 cuts). The phase was unchanged in 280/280 cuts. Over 30 process kills, elapsed time never went backwards and the recipe always resumed. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.5: Every outage is recorded (280/280). With a trusted clock, the duration is reported for 140/140 outages and is within 59 s of the truth. Without a clock, the duration is reported for only 15/140 and is unknown for the rest. Logged temperature on return is within 0.65 C of true air, and logged peak within 0.69 C. A POWER_OUTAGE alarm is info for short outages, warning for longer ones, and critical when the cabinet returns warm (for example 19.1 C at a 13 C setpoint after 6 h). (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.7: Alarms and suspicions did not change regulation. In fault-free runs where a false TEMP_READING_DISAGREES fired, compressor duty was the same in the 6 h before and after (0.91 vs 0.89, and 0.85 vs 0.84). The other side of this: after the controller's own critical "reading may be low" alarm, it kept heating on the drifting reading, up to 97 % heater duty, and true air went to 37.6 C at a 13 C setpoint. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.8: Storage full and storage write failure each raise a STORAGE_FAULT warning at 61 s on 36/36 plants, and control carries on unchanged. Torn writes were tested in the 400-case cut matrix with 0 lines lost. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.9: Temperature reading drift (plausible but wrong) is caught late or not at all. At about 1 C/h low drift at 13 C, 33/36 plants are detected at a median of 5.0 h, when true air is +5.1 C (worst +8.9 C) and product +3.7 C. Air then keeps rising to a median peak of 37.6 C (worst 42.3 C), with product at 34.7 C (worst 35.9 C). Mid-rate drift: median 57 h, 3/36 undetected. Slow drift: median 8.6 days at 13 C, 10/36 undetected; at 4 C, 8/36 undetected. Reading drifting high: median detection 5-6 h with true air -6.0 C (worst -10.6 C), air down to -11.3 C median and -20.5 C worst, product down to -19.2 C. RH drift low: median detection 21 h (fast) or 6.6 days (slow), 4/36 and 10/36 undetected, with true RH reaching 100 %. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.10: Other plausible-but-wrong readings. A stuck temperature reading is detected at median 748 s and worst 1 722 s (critical, 36/36); stuck RH at median 1 731 s and worst 4 601 s. Stuck airflow is detected at 301 s and a stuck scale at 605 s. Spikes on temperature, RH, airflow and scale, and implausible values, are detected on 36/36 plants within 5-395 s. Compressor running with no cooling, or the cooling output stuck off, is detected on 34/36 plants at median 48 min and worst 117 min, with true air +4.8 C at the latest detection. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R4.11: No rating, certification or listing status appears anywhere in the evidence. (NOT-SHOWN)
- Item R5.1: About 40 alarm codes appear in the runs, as info, warning or critical. They cover temperature high, critical and low; absolute limits (30 C high, near freezing); RH high, low and cannot fall; sensor dead, suspect, erratic, drift and disagreement; reference disagreement; cooling has no effect; cooling stuck on; heater no effect and stuck on; humidifier no effect; airflow low, blocked and suspect; fan stall and tach; door open and door switch fault; output fault and outputs dropped; storage; power outage; clock untrusted; weight step and scale; defrost cut short; cure finished; calibration check due. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R5.2: Delays and thresholds as observed on the reading. TEMP_HIGH is raised after the reading has been at least +2 C above setpoint for at least about 890 s (median dwell at +2 C is 2 690 s), and is never seen below +2.15 C. TEMP_LOW is raised after about 895 s at least 2 C below. TEMP_ABS_HIGH is raised when the reading is above 30 C. RH alarms use a ±10 %RH band (journal limits 65 at a 55 setpoint and 75 at 85), raised after roughly 150-1 150 s past ±10. DOOR_OPEN is raised at 301 s. All of these are judged on the reading, so they cannot fire while the reading drifts with the cabinet. (INFERRED)
- Item R5.3: DOOR_OPEN (warning) is raised at 301 s of an open door and clears on closing. A door switch stuck open is reported at 301 s. A dead door switch is detected at 31 s on 31/31 plants that have a switch; the 5 plants without one were not tested. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R5.4: No temperature alarm was raised during door openings of up to 60 min (true air +2.9 C on the nominal plant) or during defrosts. Defrost and door suppression appear only as test names (TestDoorSuppressionWindow, TestAlarmDelaySuppressionAndDifferential). (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R5.5: Buzzer and acknowledge. Silencing stops the buzzer for 30 min, then it sounds again while the condition persists. Acknowledging stops the buzzer. With a critical alarm still active, the contact stays signalling after acknowledge, and the buzzer sounds again 3 600 s later. With only warnings active, acknowledge releases the contact while the warning is still listed. On an unacknowledged warning, the buzzer stops by itself after 1 800 s while the contact stays signalling. A new alarm arriving while already signalling produces a 15 s contact release and a fresh buzzer. During an output write fault, the buzzer was silent for the whole 8 h fault on every plant, while the contact did open at 18 s. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R5.6: Condition alarms clear by themselves when the condition ends. The sensor fault clears in 9-25 s; DOOR_OPEN clears on close. Suspicion alarms can stay up for 49 h, and RH_DRIFT_SUSPECT was cleared by a reboot (logged duration -1). Event-type criticals (OUTPUTS_DROPPED, critical POWER_OUTAGE) stay listed as active, OUTPUTS_DROPPED never clears, but contact and buzzer stop after 1 800 s without any acknowledge. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R5.7: There is one external alarm contact; the samples log it as "contact open". In powered samples it opens within about 3-20 s of a warning or critical alarm. Across 860 unpowered samples in a 14-day run, it is logged as not signalling (0), which conflicts with the design claim in a test name (TestContactQuietInNormalRunAndOpensOnPowerLoss). (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R5.8: Events are stored in a journal with sequence number, boot count, monotonic time, wall time, clock-trusted flag, peak, limit, unit and duration. Across 30 kills, 280 cuts and 400 cut-matrix cases there were 0 bad lines, 0 sequence gaps and 0 durable lines lost. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item R5.10: CAL_CHECK_DUE (info, "Sensor check due") appears at 7 days (604 801 s) in every cure run. No other maintenance reminders were observed. Lifecycle details exist only as a test name (TestReminderLifecycle). (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item RF.1: Water activity is never measured or estimated. The end-of-recipe text tells the operator to check water activity and pH with calibrated instruments. (NOT-SHOWN)
- Item RF.2: No claim that food is safe appears. At the end of a recipe, a CURE_FINISHED warning reads "Recipe finished: operator must verify", and the final state says weight loss is a yield figure that does not show the product is safe. This appears in 8/8 cure runs. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item RF.3: Indication accuracy. Displayed temperature minus true air: mean 0.00 C, p99 absolute 0.55-0.75 C, on plants without bias. Displayed RH minus true: mean about 0, but p99 absolute 8.3-18.4 %RH because the reading lags the fast RH swings. A static plant bias of ±1 C / ±5 %RH is shown uncorrected. Weight-loss indication is within -0.05 to -1.60 percentage points of the truth over 5-71 days. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item RF.4: Excursions are stored with peak, limit, unit and duration, for humidity as well as temperature. For example, RH_HIGH on biltong has peak 76.42, limit 65 and duration 19 917 s. Outages are stored with duration, temperature on return, peak and recovery time. (MEASURED-SIM)
- Item RF.5: A per-minute process record is kept, with temperature and RH (min, max, quality), airflow, weight, setpoints, cooling, heating, humidifier and defrost fractions, door, fan, active alarm count, run state, recipe, phase, elapsed times, start weight, and degree-hours above 15.6 C. It has 0 bad lines and 0 gaps over 28-71 day cures with 2-7 boots. (MEASURED-SIM)
